# Chapter V, Section 4, of this volume. Dirac's treatment of 'quantum time' will be discussed in Volume

4, Section V.4.
180Pascual Jordan was the expert on radiation theory in Born's Institute ever since he dealt with
this subject in his thesis (Jordan, 1924). In 1925 he had treated the problem of radiation in two
papers: first, in the joint work with Born on the quantum theory of aperiodic motion (Born and
Jordan, 1925a), where the authors derived dispersion-theoretical expressions for Einstein's emission
and absorption coefficients (§10, pp. 500-503); second, in a note on the thermal equilibrium between
atoms and cavity radiation (Jordan, 1925d), where he showed that the results of Pauli (1923b, 1924b)
and of Einstein and Ehrenfest (1923) remained unchanged when Bose statistics was used for the
atoms rather than the classical (Boltzmann) statistics.
