Having the vector interpretation in hand, Lande went on to establish a
mechanical or dynamical theory. To begin with, he pointed out a difficulty with
Heisenberg's assumption (c)—that the atomic core's angular momentum should
always have the direction of the vector sum of the internal (H;) and the external

of the core of a given atom had to depend on the type of multiplet considered.

even in its basic concepts, in spite of its beautiful success in explaining the
Paschen-Back effect of doublet terms' (Lande, 1923a, p. 202).764 In order to
develop an alternative theory, Lande suggested new models for doublet and

contradictions—has not been achieved' (Lande, 1923a, p. 205). This remained a
